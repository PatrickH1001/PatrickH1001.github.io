Ziqiang Patrick Huang

Assistant Professor, Teaching Stream

I am an Assistant Professor (Teaching Stream) in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo. My primary focus lies in cultivating the next generation of engineers through rigorous and modern core computing curriculum.

My technical and research interests are centered on high-performance and power-efficient computer architecture. My past work spans the areas of compiler performance optimization, architectural hardware safety, and runtime system design for dynamic resource allocation.

I received my PhD in Computer Engineering from Duke University in 2019. Prior to joining academia full-time, I gained industry perspectives working as a research intern within the Architecture Research Team at Arm.

Teaching

ECE 250 - Algorithms and Data Structures
Data structures, storage allocation, hierarchical memory performance, and algorithm analysis (Big-O notation). Implementation and engineering trade-offs in C++.
ECE 222 - Digital Computers
Computer organization, instruction set architectures, assembly language, processor data path, pipelining, and memory hierarchies.
ECE 350 - Real-time Operating Systems
Multiprogramming, scheduling, synchronization, memory management, and performance optimization for resource-constrained environments.
ECE 621 - Computer Organizations
Advanced topics in computer architecture, instruction-level parallelism, cache coherence protocols, and hardware acceleration architectures.
ECE 498A/B - Engineering Design Project
Supervising capstone design groups working at the intersection of hardware/software co-design and high-performance computing platforms.
